# Doppel

**Find two different things that look the same. Outside.**

Photograph anything outdoors, walk, then find its twin: a different kind of thing that looks the same (a tire and a donut, moss and broccoli). EmbeddingGemma 2 runs on your phone and judges how alike they look.

Work in progress for DEV Hacktoberfest 2026 Week 1 ("Touch Grass").
